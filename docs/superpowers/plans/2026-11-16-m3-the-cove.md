# M3, the Cove's Five Places: Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** the owner chose inline execution (s11, s13): superpowers:executing-plans, task by task.
> Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** The five cove places (I Golden, II Sunset, III Dusk, VIII First light, IX Sunrise) are full, living
places, each at least 85 % by my judgement against the owner's photos. That means:
- plants, trees and cliff rock where today there is a bare slab;
- new cameras at II (down in the flowers) and III (high, looking down at the lanterns);
- Dusk's lanterns and the lantern thread;
- First light's mist;
- the life layer (birds, bats, rings, petals).

**Architecture:**
- **Plants are made in the browser, not downloaded.** A seeded, pure scatter (`src/world/scatter.ts`) places
  tufts and flowers around each cove camera by the rules of coast.py's `build_grass` (zones by noise, slope and
  height limits, drifts). Small procedural meshes are drawn in one instanced draw per kind. Cost: 0 bytes.
- **Trees and cliff rock come from Blender as meshes,** like the arch and stacks (D29), and travel in a new cove
  pack `public/world/stops/cove/`. They use the shared rock material and one foliage material (D13).
- **Lanterns, mist and life are small parts** in the glow and sprite materials. The lantern thread is state in
  `src/journey/` (pure, tested) that the lantern parts read.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript + three.js 0.186 (`three/webgpu`, `three/tsl`), Vitest, Playwright; Python 3 + numpy;
Blender 5.2 for the tree and cliff exports; adb + CDP for the phone.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-realtime-world-design.md` §5, §9.5–9.8 and the M3 row, as amended by
`2026-11-11-world-design-amendment-1.md` §3, §4 (I, II, III, VIII, IX), §6, §10. Look: `research/m3-cove-references.md`
(this milestone's Research phase), then `research/owner-references.md` (D28). Behaviour: `research/reference-book.md`
§2, §4, §7, §8, §10 (D24). Ideas: `research/inspiration-and-ideas.md` A and C. Three.js: `research/threejs-r186-notes.md`.

## Global Constraints

- **Speed bar (D9, D30):**
  - 60 fps at stops on the default tier; no frame over 50 ms in a flight;
  - the cove pack ≤ 5 MB with the headland pack included (the headland is 1.9 MB, so the cove has ≤ 3.1 MB);
  - JavaScript ≤ 400 kB gzip (now 281.5).
- **Census (D13):** ≤ 60 draws including the mirror pass, ≤ 14 materials, ≤ 2.5 M triangles at every stop.
- Plants and trees draw only near the cove stops: `visible = false` from the far third of a flight onward.
  Plants are not drawn in the mirror pass, unless the mirror is measured to need them.
- Tier knobs change density only (grass, leaf cards). They never change composition (spec §13.1). Density
  changes only while the camera rests (spec: switching cost 128–287 ms).
- **Every look check has three columns:** mid, **low** (this laptop's real tier, s12) and the owner's photo.
  Two scores: look % and behaviour %.
- Every constant for motion or colour cites a line in `reference-book.md` or a numbered owner photo.
- A changed camera is shown beside the old one and named in the session log (Amendment §2).
- No new npm dependencies. Commit on `world`; never push without asking; no AI attribution in commits.
- D15: I judge each piece (likes, dislikes, look %, behaviour %) before the owner tests anything.

## Review Focus

1. **Plants in a camera's face or in a flight path.** A tuft within 0.6 m of the camera's eye, or one standing in
   the sea or on a sheer face.
   Test (Task 1): a scatter test over every cove stop. Every instance has ground ≥ 0.3 m above sea, slope < 0.95,
   and distance to the eye ≥ the stop's lens clear.
2. **Wind that disagrees.** Grass waving over a mirror sea (Golden, First light) or still grass in Sunset's gusts.
   Test (Task 1): the sway amplitude is a pure function of `world.beaufort`, with unit tests at B0, B1 and B3.
3. **Plant cost on the laptop at low and mid, and on the phone.** Test (Task 1, Task 9): the measured fps at the
   five stops, at mid and low, and on the phone over USB.
4. **A new camera breaks a flight** (II and III move). Test (Task 4): `npm run world:test` green, flight
   clearance ≥ 2 m, `check_paths` passes.
5. **The lantern thread loses the visitor's lantern** (it is claimed at I, then must be the same one at III,
   VIII and IX). Test (Task 5): a unit test on the thread's state across stops and on a reload.

### Task 1: The ground cover (grass, meadow, flowers in drifts)

**Files:** create `src/world/scatter.ts`, `src/world/meadow.ts`, `tests/unit/scatter.test.ts`,
`tests/unit/meadow.test.ts`; modify `src/app/boot.ts`, `src/world/partOrder.ts`.

**Decisions:**
- **Placement** follows coast.py `build_grass` (the renders):
  - candidates thin out with distance, `dist = 2 + R·u^2.2`, inside the camera's yaw ±80°;
  - a plant needs ground > 0.8 m (Sunrise 0.3), slope < 0.95 and land ≥ 7 m from the shore line;
  - zones come from noise: bare patches, tall grass and short meadow, flower drifts (`fl > 0.58`) and the flower
    kind by a slow noise, so colour comes in bands (`meadow` 5, 9);
  - near-flower drifts fill the first 2.5–18 m (Sunset 900, Sunrise 900, Dusk 500).
- **Kinds:**
  - tall tuft, 0.35–1.0 m, 18–40 blades;
  - short meadow, 0.10–0.36 m;
  - three flowers: thrift pink 10–22 cm, yellow, white; each a stalk plus a head card with alpha test.
  - Procedural geometry, no textures.
- **Wind** (ref §2): `sway = A(beaufort) · gust(x − v·t)`. A(0) is tip tremble only (2 cm), A(3) is about
  0.25 rad at the tips. The gust front moves along the hour's wind direction at 3 m/s. Blades bend away, then
  spring back faster.
- **Light** (ref §8): blades facing a low sun behind them take a yellow-green rim; front-lit they are duller and
  darker. Shadow floor from `light.ts`.
- **Density per tier:** low 0.4, mid 0.7, high 1.0 (spec §13). Shown at the five cove stops; hidden from the far
  third of each flight on.
- **Pointer parting** (spec §10) with a mouse only (owner, s02). Deferred if it costs more than 0.5 ms.

**Interfaces — produces:**
- `scatterAround(stop: CoveStop, heightAt, rules): Float32Array` (x, y, z, yaw, scale, kind per instance);
- `createMeadow(scatters, light): WorldPart`;
- `swayAmplitude(beaufort): number`.

- [ ] **Step 1:** write the failing tests: Review Focus 1 (every instance on land, slope, clear of the eye);
  determinism (same seed, same bytes); flowers in drifts (a nearest-neighbour test shows clustered same-kind
  flowers, not even ones); `swayAmplitude` at B0, B1 and B3.
- [ ] **Step 2:** implement; wire in boot (joins with the opening stop's wave).
- [ ] **Step 3:** compare at the five stops, mid, low and photo; draws and triangles from the census; fps at the
  stops (mid, low). Verdict.
- [ ] **Step 4:** commit `feat(world): the cove's grass and flower drifts, made in the browser`.

### Task 2: The cliff columns and the grove

**Files:** modify `tools/world/blender/export_parts.py`, `coast.py`, `tools/world/test_parts.py`,
`src/assets/manifest.ts`, `src/app/boot.ts`; create `src/world/cliffs.ts`, `src/world/grove.ts`, tests.

**Decisions:**
- **Cliffs:** coast.py `cliff_rocks` (Golden x ≈ 22, Sunrise x ≈ −102) are exported with `rock_column` through
  the stacks path. They join the stacks' mesh family in the shared rock material (one draw).
  Grass caps come from Task 1's scatter on the tops.
- **Grove:** the `build_grove` and `build_lgrove` spots, plus Sunset's tree. Poly Haven `island_tree_01..03` and
  `searsia_burchellii` are reduced in Blender to:
  - simplified wood (≤ 3 k triangles a tree);
  - leaf cards (≤ 600 a tree, alpha test, one shared atlas ≤ 512 KB WebP).
- **Front light** (spec §9.7, research `milestones-m3-m10.md` M3):
  - each card's normal is bent toward the crown's centre, 70 % sphere and 30 % own, baked per vertex in export;
  - the back-face flip is undone in the shader.
- **Wind:** twigs only (ref §8), amplitude from Beaufort.
- Budget: cove pack ≤ 3.1 MB.

- [ ] **Step 1:** failing tests:
  - `test_parts.py`: the cove export is byte-stable, triangles are within budget, and every tree stands on the
    ground ±0.3 m;
  - unit: the bent normals point out of the crown.
- [ ] **Step 2:** export, implement, wire (the cove pack loads after the base, like the headland).
- [ ] **Step 3:** compare (mid, low, photo); Sunrise front-lit crowns read round. Verdict.
- [ ] **Step 4:** commit.

### Task 3: First light's mist

**Files:** create `src/world/mist.ts`, `tests/unit/mist.test.ts`; modify `boot.ts`.

**Decisions:**
- Try first the built-in height-fog route (`exponentialHeightFogFactor`, notes §2.5). It must be limited to
  First light (and to Sunrise, thinning): a flat band 1–2 m deep over the water, its top ≤ 2.5 m, coloured by the
  sky's horizon (`mist` 8; ref §4).
- If a scene-wide fog node costs or leaks into other stops, use a few large soft sprites lying flat instead.
- The mirror shows the band. Drift ≤ 0.1 m/s.
- The surf foam at First light is turned down to force 0–1 (today it is a bright white band).

- [ ] **Steps:** failing test (mist density 0 outside First light and Sunrise; top height ≤ 2.5 m); implement;
  compare; verdict; commit.

### Task 5: Dusk's lanterns and the lantern thread

**Files:** create `src/world/lanterns.ts`, `src/journey/thread.ts`, tests; modify `src/world/lantern.ts`, `boot.ts`.

**Decisions:**
- **The paper look** (video 4): pale cream at the top, orange only low where the candle is; soft edges, no hard
  dark frame; a thin dark float.
- **Dusk:** 44 lanterns along coast.py's path ((−12, 52) → (−150, 190)), bunched in loose strings. They drift
  ≤ 0.03 m/s with the hour's wind and turn slowly. They light one by one during the arrival, over about 4 s.
  One instanced draw; streaks in the mirror.
- **The thread** (ideas A):
  - I: hover or tap claims the lantern;
  - III: it is among the drifting lanterns, slightly warmer;
  - VIII: it rests in the shallows, dim;
  - IX: it rests there too, and the sun takes over.
  - Kept in `sessionStorage` (wrapped in try/catch).
- The thread's later places (IV–VII) belong to their milestones.

- [ ] **Steps:** failing tests (Review Focus 5; drift speed ≤ 0.03 m/s; the lighting order); implement; compare;
  verdict; commit.

## Order and sessions

This milestone is larger than one session. The order puts the biggest visual gain first: Task 1 (every place
gains), Task 2 (the slab goes), Task 3, Task 4, Task 5, Task 6, Task 7, Task 8. Every task ends with a commit and
a verdict, so a session can stop between tasks.
