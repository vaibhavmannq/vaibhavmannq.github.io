# M5, Night: Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** the owner chose inline execution (s11, s13, s17, s18: "complete M5 in this session"):
> superpowers:executing-plans, task by task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** Night (V) is rebuilt as Amendment 1 §5 says, at least 85 % by my judgement against the owner's photos and
the three real places:
- a narrow, wet, mossy bowl of basalt columns, open to the sky (Gljúfrabúi, Svartifoss, Litlanesfoss);
- a two-tier fall, split into ribbons, lit only by the sky and the aurora;
- the aurora overhead (rays converging, a corona) and one band low over the open side, doubled in the lagoon;
- fireflies that drift toward the pointer; glowing reed tips in the near shallows;
- the project lanterns on the water: hover lifts and brightens, click opens a card, each with an HTML twin.

**Architecture:**
- **No download.** The bowl, the reeds, the fireflies and the corona's curtains are made in the browser from seeded,
  pure, tested data (`src/world/bowl.ts`, `nightlife.ts`, `corona.ts`), like Blue hour's caps.
- **One rock material (D13).** The bowl is real hexagonal prisms (silhouettes: stepped tops, fallen drums) in one
  merged mesh drawn with the shared rock material, which already reads `light`, `moss` and `column` per vertex.
  Each prism's `column` cancels the material's lean so the per-pixel pattern shades the prism as one column, while
  its drum cuts fall across it. Moss is that material's mask. The lagoon is fresh water: no barnacle line there
  (`rock.ts` `seaWetNode`, already used by the ground).
- **The falls keep M2's shader layers** (glassy lip, white break, pulses, spray); only the geometry changes: a list of
  tiers, each a sheet of ribbons, stepping down a ledge.
- **The corona replaces Night's baked curtain lines** (export_parts.py's) with curtains made in `corona.ts`: rays run
  up meridians on the sky sphere, so they converge at the zenith by construction. Midnight's curtains do not change.
- **The camera moves** (Amendment §2: renders no longer decide cameras): coast.py `SHOTS["night"]` cam/look/lens,
  exported by `export_hours.py`; `?cams=old` gains Night's old camera (`OLD_CAMS`).
- **The projects** come from `content/projects.ts` (three today) plus the visitor's lantern (the thread). The card is
  a plain `<dialog>`; its words and style are M9's.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript + three.js 0.186 (`three/webgpu`, `three/tsl`), Vitest, Playwright; adb + CDP for the phone.

**Spec:** `2026-11-10-realtime-world-design.md` §2 (V), §10 and the M5 row, as amended by
`2026-11-11-world-design-amendment-1.md` §2, §3, §5, §6. Look: `research/m5-night-references.md` (this milestone's
Research phase), then `research/owner-references.md` §2, §3, §11, §12 (D28). Behaviour: `research/reference-book.md`
§3, §5, §6, §7 (D24). Ideas: `research/inspiration-and-ideas.md` (fireflies round the pointer, the lantern thread).
Land: `research/reference-landforms.md` §1, §2. Three.js: `research/threejs-r186-notes.md`.

## Global Constraints

- **Speed bar (D9, D30):** 60 fps at the stop on the default tier; no frame over 50 ms in a flight; no new download.
- **Census (D13):** ≤ 60 draws including the mirror pass, ≤ 14 materials, ≤ 2.5 M triangles at every stop. Night
  today: 34 draws, 1.15 M triangles, 8 materials.
- **Phone path:** nothing essential may depend on compute (WebGL2 must draw it).
- **Night's light (book §3, §5):** the falls as bright as the sky lighting them; shadows deep teal, never black;
  only wet faces and moss take the aurora's green.
- **Build hygiene:** after any source edit, rebuild before shooting and check the build log's last line. Never
  measure while `world:test` runs. A/B one page at a time (`.superpowers/m3/tools/ab-builds.mjs`,
  `.superpowers/m4/tools/ab-shot.mjs`). Check every part joined (`.superpowers/m3/tools/joined.mjs`).
- **Python edits** of source files: `newline=''`.
