## How a round works

1. **The owner tests** the latest build on his own devices, as a visitor would: laptop (Chrome, mouse) and phone (Brave,
   touch), sound on and off, scrolling, the Next button, look-around, the spots, the project lanterns, a portrait phone.
2. **The owner gives his experience,** in chat or in the template below: per place and per flight what he likes, what
   he dislikes, and anything that felt wrong to do (slow, confusing, broken, too fast, too dark, too loud). A rough
   score per place helps but is not needed.
3. **I triage it** into a numbered list in the ledger (each item: what he said, where, what I think causes it, the fix,
   the evidence it needs), say plainly where I disagree and why (honest pushback), and order it by what a visitor meets
   first.
4. **I fix and judge** each item (measure before fixing; shots beside the old view and his photos; low beside mid;
   moving things judged in motion; a cold first ride over any flight a change touches, R38; phone over USB) and only
   hand back what I call ready (D15).
5. **He re-tests.** Repeat until a round ends with nothing he still minds.

### Feedback template (copy per round; any order, any length)

```
Round N · device (laptop / phone, browser) · sound on/off

Overall: (first feeling in a sentence)

Place by place (I Golden … IX Sunrise): likes / dislikes / score if you like
Flights (I→II … VIII→IX): likes / dislikes
Doing things (scroll, Next, look-around, spots, lanterns, sound button, loading): what felt good / bad
Sound: (each place, loudness, anything that annoys)
Ideas: (anything you want added or taken away)
```
