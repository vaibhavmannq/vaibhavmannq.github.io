# M4, Blue hour: Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** the owner chose inline execution (s11, s13, s17: "complete M4 in this session"):
> superpowers:executing-plans, task by task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** Blue hour (IV) is a full, living place, at least 85 % by my judgement against the owner's photos:
- the ankle-deep flats;
- the arch overhead;
- scrub caps on the stacks;
- plankton that lights where the water moves and answers the pointer;
- the first dense stars.

**Architecture:**
- **No download, no new material.** Plankton is a term in the sea's shader. The stars are a change in the sky's
  shader. The scrub caps are a second mesh sharing the grove's material, built from the shrub already in the cove pack.
- **One world state decides the plankton.** A new per-hour value `plankton` (0 in daylight, 1 at Blue hour) is
  blended between hours like every other value, so the sparks fade in on the flight from Dusk and out toward Night.
- **Pointer and walker are pure state.** A small ring buffer of "stir" points on the water (`src/world/stir.ts`,
  pure and tested) takes the pointer's hits and the walking camera's feet. The sea's shader reads it, the way it
  reads the fish ring.
- **The arch overhead is a flight, not a camera move.** `FLIGHT_OVERRIDES['bluehour>night']` leaves low through the
  opening and climbs round toward Night. The stop keeps its approved view.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript + three.js 0.186 (`three/webgpu`, `three/tsl`), Vitest, Playwright; adb + CDP for the phone.

**Spec:** `2026-11-10-realtime-world-design.md` §2 (IV) and the M4 row, as amended by
`2026-11-11-world-design-amendment-1.md` §3, §4 (IV), §6, §7. Look: `research/m4-blue-hour-references.md` (this
milestone's Research phase), then `research/owner-references.md` §6, §10, §13 (D28). Behaviour:
`research/reference-book.md` §1, §2, §3, §6, §8 (D24). Ideas: `research/inspiration-and-ideas.md` (plankton that
answers the pointer). Three.js: `research/threejs-r186-notes.md`.

## Global Constraints

- **Speed bar (D9, D30):** 60 fps at the stop on the default tier; no frame over 50 ms in a flight; no new
  download. Blue hour's place budget (≤ 5 MB with the headland pack) is unchanged.
- **Census (D13):** ≤ 60 draws including the mirror pass, ≤ 14 materials, ≤ 2.5 M triangles at every stop.
- **Phone path:** nothing essential may depend on compute (WebGL2 must draw it).
- **D2:** no glowing cyan water. Plankton is sparks on dark water only.
- **Build hygiene:** after any source edit, rebuild before shooting and check the build log's last line. Never
  measure while `world:test` runs. A/B one page at a time (`.superpowers/m3/tools/ab-builds.mjs`).
- **Judging (D15):** every task ends with likes, dislikes, a % for look and a % for behaviour. Moving things are
  judged in motion (frame strips), not only stills. Changed views go beside the old ones and the owner's photos,
  with a low column beside mid.

### Task 1: Plankton (book §6; owner `plankton` 1–6, videos 1–3)
**Files:** `src/world/stir.ts` (new, pure), `src/world/sea.ts`, `src/journey/hours.ts`, `src/journey/types.ts`,
`src/app/boot.ts` (pointer → stir), `tests/unit/stir.test.ts`, `tests/unit/hours.test.ts`.
- [ ] `PLANKTON: Record<StopId, number>` in hours.ts (Blue hour 1, Night 0: a freshwater lagoon; all others 0),
  blended in `blendHours`.
- [ ] `stir.ts`: a ring of 16 points (x, z, age, strength). `stir(at, now)` adds one, but only after the last one
  is 0.35 m away (a trail, not a pile). `ages(now)` returns the shader's uniform data. Fatigue: a point laid within
  1.5 m of a point younger than 1 s starts at half strength. Tests first.
- [ ] Pointer: a ray from the camera through the pointer meets the water plane (y = 0) within 60 m → stir. The
  walking camera stirs at its own feet every 0.6 m.
- [ ] Sea shader: a hashed grid of cells on the water. Each cell is one spark with its own phase.
  - Waterline sparks: where the water is shallower than 0.4 m over the 8 m grid, or within 2.5 m of the arch's or a
    stack's foot (a distance texture baked once from the rock meshes at the waterline). They flare when a slow
    ripple crest passes.
  - Ripple-crest sparks on the flats: along the crests of the longest of the six ripples.
  - Stir sparks: within 0.5–1.2 m of each stir point, bright at once, gone in about 1.5 s.
  - Sparks fade out where a cell is under 1.5 px (no far shimmer). Colour: blue (475 nm), cyan-white at the
    brightest. Not in the mirror (the sea's own shader).
- [ ] Judge: frame strips of the stop, a pointer sweep, a wade. Look and behaviour %.
