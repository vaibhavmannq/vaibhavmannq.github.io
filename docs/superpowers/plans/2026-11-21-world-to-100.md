# The night's walk at 100 % (M8.6): Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.
> **This project:** inline execution (owner, s11 onward), terse chat; each task closes with a commit, a % verdict and its numbers in the ledger.

**Goal:** every place at 100 % in all five views on low and high, and every ride a smooth, planned shot with no dead stretch,
inside relaxed limits (≈ 60 MB, a lantern loading experience) and at 60 fps.

**Architecture:** Phase 0 builds the measuring tools (journey motion, dead share, GPU ceiling) and the three structural
pieces the later phases fill: the lantern loader, the far layer that is always drawn, and close-up sets streamed and
compiled inside passages. Phases 1–3 then lift the eight causes and each place on top of those pieces; Phase 4 judges
the whole night. Phases 1–4 are written in detail at their own start, from Phase 0's numbers (the budgets they size
content to do not exist yet).

**Tech Stack:** three.js r186 (WebGPU, WebGL2 fallback), TSL, TypeScript 7, Vite 8, Vitest 5, Playwright 1.63 (quiet
Chrome, phone over CDP), Blender + Python tools in `tools/world/`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-21-world-to-100-design.md` (D53). Also binding: Amendment 1, D1–D52.

**Ledger:** `docs/handover/ledgers/2026-11-21-world-to-100.md` (rulings from R208). **Check scripts:** `.superpowers/w100/`.

## Global Constraints

- 60 fps at low on this laptop at every stop and through every ride; phone 60 fps at mid/high (D18, R198).
- Download ≈ 60 MB in all; ≈ 10 MB before the first place; the rest during rides (D53).
- The name on screen < 1 s; the lantern experience 5–15 s on a first visit, ≈ 2 s when everything is cached (spec §4.1).
- No fixed material/draw caps; whatever holds the frame rate, measured (D53 replaces D13's caps).
- R38 is law: every part compiled for the eye and the mirror before it shows; cold rides measure 0 late pipelines.
- Anti-goals: no stutter on a ride, no visible pop-in, the whole coast visible from high places, no loader that feels like waiting.
- No stills or painted backdrops (D50); look-around stays ±40° / ±15° (D27).
- Whole-world changes (sea, sky, light) checked at all nine stops and every ride before the owner sees them (D47).
- Speed verdicts from a whole-night gpu-sweep old vs new, run alone (s26); one test browser at a time, quiet (`.superpowers/quiet-browser.mjs`).
- No git stash; never push without asking; no AI attribution in commits.

## Review Focus

1. **A visitor rides backwards** (Prev, scroll up, the rail): the place behind must have its set or a clean far version, never an empty place. → Task 4 test `wantedSets going back keeps the previous place`.
2. **A visitor jumps** (rail click, `?stop=`, a fast flick across three places): the set is not prefetched; the place must show its far version and swap the set in behind a fade, not hitch. → Task 4 test `a jump with no prefetch asks for the target at once`; Task 5 test `a jump swaps behind the fade`.
3. **Reduced motion** (no flights, a cross-fade): no passage exists; the swap must happen behind the cross-fade. → Task 5 test `reduced motion swaps at the fade's midpoint`.
4. **A slow or failing network during the lantern**: the lantern must not hang; a cap moves on to the journey with whatever is ready, a hard failure goes to the stills path. → Task 6 tests `the lantern gives up waiting at the cap` and `a failed base load ends in stills`.
5. **Storage blocked** (private mode, sessionStorage throws) for the returning-visitor skip: the loader runs as a first visit and never throws. → Task 6 test `blocked storage counts as a first visit`.

### Task 1: The ride simulator and its motion numbers

**Files:**
- Create: `src/journey/ridePlay.ts`, `src/journey/motion.ts`, `tools/journey/journey_report.ts`
- Test: `tests/unit/ridePlay.test.ts`, `tests/unit/motion.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `buildTrack`, `frameAt`, `stopCentre` (timeline.ts); `rideMs`, `follow` (pace.ts); `poseAt` (camera.ts); flight paths from `src/journey/flightPaths.json`.
- Produces:
  - `simulateRide(track: Track, paths: readonly FlightPath[], from: number, to: number, opts?: { hz?: number; aspect?: number }): RideSample[]` — `RideSample = { tS: number; u: number; pose: Pose }`. Replays what a Next ride does: the scroll moves linearly over `rideMs(track, fromU, toU)`, the camera chases it with `follow` each tick, the pose is `poseAt(frameAt(...))`. Default `hz` 120, `aspect` 16/9. Drift is excluded (`timeS` fixed at 0) so it does not read as jerk.
  - `motionStats(samples: RideSample[]): RideStats` — `RideStats = { durationS; speed: {max; p50}; accel: {max}; jerk: {max; p95}; gaze: {maxDegS; maxDegS2; maxDegS3}; speedSteps: { tS: number; dv: number }[] }` (m/s, m/s², m/s³, °/s, °/s², °/s³). A speed step is a tick where |Δspeed| / dt exceeds 4 m/s² *and* the next tick's change has the same sign (a kink, not noise).

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests**
  - `motion.test.ts`: `constant speed has zero accel and jerk` (samples on a straight line at 10 m/s, 120 Hz → accel.max < 1e-6, jerk.max < 1e-6); `a velocity step is found` (10 m/s for 1 s then 20 m/s → `speedSteps` has one entry at tS ≈ 1.0, dv ≈ 10); `a gaze snap is found` (yaw jumps 30° in one tick → gaze.maxDegS ≥ 3000).
  - `ridePlay.test.ts`: `a ride starts at one stop's camera and ends at the next` (from 0 to 1: first and last pose within 0.05 m of `STOPS[0].cam` / `STOPS[1].cam` in three coordinates); `a ride lasts about rideMs` (durationS within 0.5 s of `rideMs / 1000` plus the follow's settle, settle < 1.5 s).

- [ ] **Step 3: Implement** `simulateRide` and `motionStats` (finite differences on positions and on unwrapped yaw/pitch; speed in m/s from three-coordinate positions).
- [ ] **Step 4: Run the tests** — Expected: PASS.
- [ ] **Step 5: `tools/journey/journey_report.ts`** prints one row per ride (I→II … VIII→IX): duration, speed p50/max, accel max, jerk p95/max, gaze max °/s and °/s², speed steps (count and where, as ride t). `node tools/journey/journey_report.ts` — Expected: 8 rows. ### Task 2: Dead share, screen motion and real frame pacing

**Files:**
- Create: `src/journey/sightline.ts`, `.superpowers/w100/ride-frames.mjs`
- Modify: `tools/journey/journey_report.ts` (adds the columns)
- Test: `tests/unit/sightline.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `HeightAt`, `createDrawnHeightSampler` (heights.ts) and the base grid from `public/world/base/`; `RideSample` (Task 1).
- Produces:
  - `nearestHit(pose: Pose, heightAt: HeightAt, opts?: { maxM?: number; rays?: number; aspect?: number }): number` — the median distance (m) of the ground hits of a `rays`×`rays` grid (default 5×5) over the central 60 % of the frame; `Infinity` when fewer than half hit within `maxM` (default 300).
  - `screenMotion(a: RideSample, b: RideSample, distM: number): number` — the frame-centre's image motion in screen widths per second: gaze angular speed over the horizontal fov plus lateral camera speed / `distM` over the fov.
  - `.superpowers/w100/ride-frames.mjs <outDir> [tier=low] [n=24]`: one quiet Chrome; per ride `n` frames via `__flightPose(f, t)`; per frame the edge share (Sobel on a 320-wide grey copy, share of pixels over a fixed gradient threshold of 0.08) computed in the page from the screenshot; writes `edges.json`. Then plays each ride with the Next button (`#ride`) at low and records frame intervals (p95, max, > 20 ms, > 50 ms).

- [ ] **Step 5: Extend the report:** per ride, screen motion p50/max and its ratio max/p50 (target ≤ 2), and the **dead share**: the share of time where `nearestHit` is `Infinity` **and** the edge share (from `edges.json`, nearest t) is under 0.15; the longest dead stretch in seconds (target ≤ 1).
