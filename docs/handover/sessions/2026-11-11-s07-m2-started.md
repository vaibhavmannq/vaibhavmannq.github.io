## Learned

- The lagoon lies under the cloud deck's north edge, so the flight out of it climbs through the deck's height. The
  deck therefore forms only below a camera already above it (`deckWeight`).
- coast.py's cloud crests (33 m, 0.45 x amp) read as an ice field on an 8 m grid without Blender's subsurface light;
  rounded 70 m billows at 0.26 x amp read as cloud.
- This renderer's noise is coarser than Blender's at the same scale (aurora rays needed 1.6 x).
- The baked cave light: the back wall faces the mouth and is the lit part; rock beside the mouth is dark.
- The dev server reloads the page after source edits and the reload can land on Golden: look checks must confirm
