## Learned

- `|noise|` draws lines at its zero crossings: it gave cloud "veins" and cave "squiggles". Shade from the noise's
  height for lumps.
- three's `ReflectorNode` hides whichever material triggers it; a second user of the mirror gets black.
- A TSL `If` must sit inside `Fn`, or stage B fails silently ("reading 'If'" of null) and the world never loads.
- Per-pixel noise over a full screen is what costs; per-vertex varyings are nearly free here.
- The dev server's reload after a source edit lands the first page load wrong (?stop / ?p lost); always shoot twice
  after an edit, or use the prod build.
- The 10 Mbit/s load test fails when the machine is busy (2.4 s); alone it passes 3/3.
