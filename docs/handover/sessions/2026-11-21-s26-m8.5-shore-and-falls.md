## Learned
- A plan-view fix is not a look fix: the rounded terrace still read as a cake from the cameras until it got a rolling
  top and columns on its faces. Judge in the frames, every time.
- Masks that mean "the land ends here" can reach across the whole world: diff `ground_base` old/new on a coarse
  world grid after every outline change (`tmp far.py` pattern; 20 m over ±6 km takes seconds).
- The left headland's north shore sits ~1 m above the sea: any edge moved there makes slivers and horns. Round corners
  with a true rounded intersection (`_round_inside`), not by shifting one edge.
- Closer, taller rock costs GPU per pixel (+1.1 ms at Golden). Drawing rock before the ground cost more, not less.
- Moving a plant "up onto the land" can put it on a camera's line to its subject: test the subject lines.
- Checking one part on/off is not a speed verdict: run the whole-night sweep old vs new before saying "same speed".
- Don't run tests or bakes while a GPU measurement runs on this laptop: it doubles the noise.
- A steep rise in coast.py moves a cell inward on the live 8 m grid: shoot with parts hidden to tell ground from rock.
