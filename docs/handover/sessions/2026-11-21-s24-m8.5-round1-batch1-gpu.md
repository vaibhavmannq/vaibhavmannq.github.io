## Learned
- A phone's GPU clock slows when the work is light: GPU timer queries on a phone measure its power state, not its
  capacity. Judge phones by frame rate under load.
- A canvas at `opacity: 0` is never presented, so frame timing behind it says nothing.
- When the poster lifts, the rest of the world is still loading: the worst moment to measure anything.
- Playwright's `connectOverCDP` can hang on Brave (a target with an empty URL); a plain WebSocket to the page target works
  (`.superpowers/m85/phone-night-cdp.mjs`, also `CDP=9333 HOST=127.0.0.1` for the laptop's quiet Chrome).
