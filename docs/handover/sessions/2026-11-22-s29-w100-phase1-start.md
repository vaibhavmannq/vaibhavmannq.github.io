## Learned
- `scheduler.yield()` continuations run ahead of a due frame in Chrome: to let a frame draw between slices, post a
  message (MessageChannel) instead.
- three r186 TAAU with an MRT velocity breaks additive / transparent materials built after compileAsync returns
  (their shaders miss the second target); camera-only motion from depth avoids it and keeps R38's keys.
- `gpu-sweep.mjs` resets quality per config: a URL `scale=` does nothing there; it now has an "ultra (today)" config.
- The phone after ~15 min of sweeps is hot: a visitor's night falls to low by VI→VII on old and new builds alike;
  judge phone rides cool.
- A high-passed detail texture has no patches: macro variation needs its own low-frequency pattern.
