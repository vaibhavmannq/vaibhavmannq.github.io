# The owner's eight photos (s25, 2026-11-21): what he keeps choosing

Shared in chat during M8.5 Round 1 ("one of the most beautiful photos I have seen"). Seen as screenshots only; the
originals are not in `.superpowers/refs/` yet (asked). Credits as shown on the images.

## The yardstick (all eight agree)
1. **Layered depth with air between layers:** near, middle, far, each paler and bluer than the one in front.
2. **Light raking from the side:** every ridge and rock has a lit side and a shadow side; never flat or fully backlit.
3. **Rock with real texture:** tilted plates, strata bands, broken edges catching light; never a smooth sheet.
4. **Water that moves around things:** wash streaming between rocks and back, foam hugging the rocks' feet.

Judge every remaining Round 1 item against these four.
