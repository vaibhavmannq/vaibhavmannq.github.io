### Round 1 fixes

**#1 · the sea alive (R162).** A long swell from the depth field: crests whose phase grows with √depth (shallow-water
speed), so they slow, close up and turn to follow each shore by themselves; shoaling (Green's law) until they break at
1.4 × their height as a foam bore (hard front, trail behind); the sea's level rises and falls with the wash (a quick
run-up, a slow slide back, ~2 m up a 1:20 beach). Height per place (`SWELL_M`), 0 in the Night lagoon; 8 s period.
- A first try took the depth's slope from the bilinear grid in the shader: every 8 m cell edge showed as a straight fold
  in the reflections. Now the depth is smoothed and its slope made once on the CPU (`swellTexture`, one RGBA16F texel).
- Cost: GPU ms per frame at Golden low, median of three interleaved runs (`?gpu`, `__hold`): old 11.4, new 11.3, new with
  the swell off 11.5. Nothing measurable. Frame-time runs on this laptop swing between 1 and 20 slow frames per 10 s in
  either build (memory 2.1 GB free).
- Verdict: look 75 %, behaviour 72 %. Likes: the waterline runs up and back at Golden, First light and Sunrise; the swell
  rolls through the reflections as broad bands; Dusk and Night stay mirror-calm. Dislikes: the open water far out
  still reads still (the plane is flat; no crests stand up); the bore is a soft pale band rather than white water.

**#2 (a), first try, dropped (s23).** Steep ground drawn as layered rock (sine beds and joints in the terrain shader).
Golden's right wall barely changed (a flat face in shadow stays flat whatever its colour) and Sunrise's left slope
showed a fine crosshatch like a wire mesh. Reverted. Next: real shape (browser-made rock columns along the bare walls,
as `passage.ts` makes the cave's), plus plants in the side wedges.

**#2, measured (s23).** Plants per 15° wedge, 5–60 m, and the ground at 10/25/45 m along each wedge (height/slope;
`.superpowers/m85/diag.txt`): the bare sides are steep faces close in, slope 2.5 (~68°) at 10–25 m: Golden's right
(+45°), Dusk's left (−15…−60°), Sunrise's left (−15…−60°). Plants stop at slope 1.3 (`TURF_SLOPE`), so these faces
stay plain 8 m ground with one smeared colour. First light's sides are water and sand (none needed). Shots of all nine
stops at ±40° at low: `look.mjs` (fixed: a drag that starts off the window does nothing).
**Plan for #2 (next session):** a new part `crags.ts`: for each stop, sample the ground in a ±55° cone at 6–70 m, and
where the slope is over 1.3, stand rock prisms against the face (the bowl's prism builder and the shared rock material,
