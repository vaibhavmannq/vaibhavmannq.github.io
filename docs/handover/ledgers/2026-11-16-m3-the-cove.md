**Task 1, the ground cover.** Commit 2f3d80a.
- `scatter.ts` places plants by coast.py `build_grass`'s rules, with its noise ported line for line (two values
  checked against numpy).
- Kinds: tall tufts, short meadow mats, and thrift, yellow and white flower clumps in drifts.
- `meadow.ts` draws them with one material and 3 instanced draws per stop.
- No download.
- Tested: every plant on land, slope and eye clearance, seeded, flowers in drifts (nearest flower is the same kind
  > 70 %), sway by Beaufort.

Rulings:
- **R39:** turf grows to slope 1.3 (52°), tall tufts to coast.py's 0.95.
  - Why: on the 8 m base, Dusk's and Sunrise's turf slopes are 45–50°, and coast.py's limit left them bare.
  - Cost if wrong: grass on a few faces that should be bare.
- **R40:** each cove stop draws only its own plants. The plants of the stop left behind shrink away over the second
  half of the flight; those ahead grow in over the first half, while the camera is still far from them.
  - Measured: drawing all 37 k plants at every cove stop cost about 1.5 ms of GPU time on mid and low alike
    (vertex-bound).
  - Per stop it cost 0.1–0.2 ms on low and about 1.3 ms on mid.
  - Cost if wrong: thinner far meadow where two stops' views overlap.
- **R41:** the plants stay out of the water's mirror.
  - The mirror's camera, cloned from the eye's, drops `MEADOW_LAYER` (sea.ts wraps r186
    `ReflectorBaseNode.getVirtualCamera`).
  - Cost if wrong: no grass reflected where the meadow meets still water.

Counts (high): 37 117 plants, 0.76 M triangles (tall 27, mat 16, flower clump 48 each).

## Session s14 (2026-11-16), Opus 5.5, inline

**Task 2, the cliff columns and the grove.**
- `coast.py`: the grove's spots are pure data above the render setup (`GROVE_SPOTS`, `LGROVE_SPOTS`, `grove_plants()`:
  43 trees and shrubs, the renders' RNG order kept). `build_grove`, `build_lgrove` and Sunrise's props now place them.
- `export_parts.py export_cove` (run by `npm run world:blender`, golden hour) writes the cove pack
  `public/world/stops/cove/`:
  - `cliffs.bin` (570 KB): the 24 `cliff_rocks` columns (Golden x ≈ 22, Sunrise x ≈ −102) built by `rock_column`
    with the renders' seeds, subdivided once, decimated to 47 k triangles, the arch's layout; their sky light baked
    with the ground behind them blocking rays.
  - `grove.bin` (184 KB): the four Poly Haven models as wood (island trees 34 k → 2.4 k triangles) and leaf cards
    (500 per tree, 600 for the three-shrub searsia model), plus the 43 placements.
  - `foliage.py` (pure numpy): leaves grouped on a grid into cards; each card's normal bent 70 % toward the crown's
    dome (centre 30 % up the crown, never leaning down more than 0.45), 30 % its own facing.
  - Re-export is byte-identical (sha1 compared).
- Browser: `cove.ts` (pack, where it is in play), `cliffs.ts` (shared rock material, one draw), `grove.ts` (one
  geometry, one material, one draw; light per vertex from the bent normals as the meadow's; leaf atlas drawn on a
  canvas; twigs sway by Beaufort; a tier knob `leaves`: low 0.8).
- Tests: `test_parts.py` (leaf cards, cliffs, grove), `tests/unit/grove.test.ts` (decode, Blender's rotation order,
  roots on the live ground, bent normals out of each crown after placement, tier order, framing within 400 m,
  6 m clearance from the cliffs and 2 m from any leaf for every camera). `framing()` gained a `reach`.
- Cove pack 0.75 MB; Golden's place 2.87 MB of 5. JS 286.9 kB gz.

Rulings:
- **R43:** plants stand on the live 8 m height grid, not on coast.py's formula; a plant whose grid ground is under
  0.8 m is left out (6 of 43: two at (−47, 132) would stand in 18 m of water).
  - Why: along the rims the grid lies 2–5 m under the formula (it smooths the drop), and the meadow and terrain follow
    the grid.
  - Cost if wrong: Sunset's grove has two trees fewer (checked: the tree line does not read thin).
- **R44:** the searsia shrub has no wood: its stems are thousands of loose twigs that do not simplify (16.8 k
  triangles left at a 1.5 k target), and its leaves hide them. Cost if wrong: a shrub seen from under 5 m shows no stems.
- **R45:** the leaf atlas is drawn in the browser (sprigs of pointed leaves on a canvas), not a 512 KB WebP.
  Cost if wrong: crowns less like the Poly Haven leaves up close.
- **R46:** the cove parts are drawn whole on Midnight → Before dawn and Before dawn → First light (both frame the cove
  within 400 m: the framing test), and on Dusk → Blue hour only over its first two thirds.
- **R47:** a cliff column whose top clears the live ground by under 2.5 m is lowered to 0.5 m under it (`cliff_columns`).
  - Why: the right headland's columns stand as its face with land behind; from Sunrise one showed as a bright moss
    blob in the turf.
  - Cost if wrong: Golden's columns are 1–2 m shorter than in the render.
- **R48:** the column tops get the shared material's moss caps, not Task 1's tufts (the scatter only knows the
  height grid). Cost if wrong: bare-looking tops up close.
- **R49:** the trees take no shadows and cast none; they are drawn in the water's mirror. Cost: Task 8 measures it.

**Task 5, Dusk's lanterns, the thread, the paper look.**

- `lanterns.ts`: Dusk's 44 lanterns on coast.py's path, about half following the one before as loose strings,
  ordered from the cove outward; one instanced draw for the paper and float, one for their streaks on the water.
  - They light one by one over 3.5 s as the visitor arrives (and again on the next arrival), drift ≤ 0.03 m/s with

  at Sunrise. Kept in sessionStorage; blocked storage, no storage or a bad value read as unclaimed.
- Tests: `thread.test.ts` (Review Focus 5: places per stop, reload, blocked storage), `lanterns.test.ts` (on open

Rulings:

  - Cost if wrong: one more draw at Dusk (43 → 45); nothing measurable in GPU time.
