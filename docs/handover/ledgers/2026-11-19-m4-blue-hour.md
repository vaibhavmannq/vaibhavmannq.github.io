### Task 1: plankton (f950f6b, ce3042a, 85f948c)
- **R76:** sea sparkle is one sprite draw (`plankton.ts`, its own material), eye only, not a term in the sea's shader:
  a spark on a plane seen at 0.4 m eye height would be squashed to a line, and a 3×3 cell search cannot find sparks
  stretched 25× along the view.
- Three kinds:
  - the waterline where the arch's and stacks' triangles cross z = 0 (`waterline()`, thinned to one per 0.35 m,
    2 400 kept), flaring a beat after each crest of the sea's longest ripple (10.5 m, 4.3 s), 65 % of crests;
  - 1 600 rare sparks on the flats, each on a 6–20 s clock, on a 40 m tile round the eye;
  - 40 sparks round each of 24 stir points.
- **R77, stir points** (`stir.ts`, pure, tested): the pointer's ray meets the water within 60 m (spacing 0.35 m, or 3 %
  of the distance); a finger while it is down; the wading feet a stride ahead of the eye (strength 1.4, a wider ring,
  a longer wake). Water stirred again within 4 s is at 0.4 (fatigue; 4 s is my call, the sources give no number).
- `PLANKTON` per hour: Blue hour 1, every other hour 0 (Night's lagoon is fresh water); blended on the flights.
- Reduced motion: only the visitor's stirs light, without flicker (final review).

### Task 2: stars (5520217, 23646a3)
- **R78:** one candidate per cell (330 cells/rad), flux u^−0.93 (2.7× more per magnitude), shown only where it
  outshines the sky's own luminance; air dims it up to 2 magnitudes near the horizon; size from flux, never under a
  pixel (fwidth); colour by temperature; twinkle 30 % at the horizon, 4 % overhead. Visibility constant 0.0002 gives
  about 1 % of cells at Night. The field sits in a uniform branch (`If(u.stars > 0.01)`, inside `Fn`): daylight hours
  skip it.

### Task 3: scrub caps (43afdaa, 23646a3)

- **R80:** caps draw only at Blue hour and its two flights (`capsInPlay`): from the cove they are specks and cost

### Task 4: the flats (228b5b9, ce3042a, 4930a40)
