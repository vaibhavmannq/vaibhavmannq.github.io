choices (new cameras, `?cams=old`) until the final review session. Night before M5 (`before/mid.png`): the 8 m height

### Task 1: the bowl and the camera (0649242)
- **R84:** the bowl is made in the browser (`bowl.ts`, pure, seeded, tested), not exported from Blender: hexagonal
  prisms are a few lines, cost no download (D30) and no render round is needed for stills any more (D27). About 1 900
  prisms: six rows (the front row broken at 6–50 % of the rim, then 30–85 %, 60–100 %, 80–100 %, two at the rim),
  runs of six columns breaking together, buttresses every 15–20 m, a band of short leaning columns on the rim,
  360 fallen drums in a talus at the foot (none in the pool, none within 4.5 m of the eye). One mesh, ~31 k
  triangles, the shared rock material (D13).
- **R85:** each prism's `column` attribute carries its own centre minus the material's lean with height, so the
  per-pixel column pattern shades the prism as one column while its drum cuts still fall across it. No new material.
- **R86:** the lagoon is fresh water: the shared rock material reads the lagoon (`seaWetNode`), so the bowl wears a
  dark wet band but no barnacle line. The lagoon's centre is now the bowl's (was halfway between pool and camera).
- **R87, the open side:** the land outside the crater is 40–55 m high everywhere except the west (21–25 m), so the
  sky can only open there. coast.py lowers the crater's rim by 62 % in a 40° band centred on azimuth 285°
  (to about 12 m); the base was re-baked (`bake_base.py`, `bake_flights.ts`, `bake_dressing.py`, manifest). Night's old
  view is added to `views.KEPT_VIEWS` so the M1 keep is unchanged; `test_keep.py` and `test_views.py` pass.
- **R88, the camera** (coast.py; Amendment §2): at azimuth 175° from the bowl's centre, 37 m out, eye 1 m over the
  water (`over_water`: the export takes the water, not the lagoon's floor, as the ground); look −19°, pitch +2°, lens
  20. The buttress (azimuth 192°, 11 m deep) rises out of frame on the left 10 m away; the falls 86 m off, a third in
  from the right at a three-quarter angle; the open west side centre-left. Before: cam (−320, −437, 0.9), look (0, 12).
  `?cams=old` keeps the old view and leaves the bowl out (the old camera stands behind the new south wall).
- **R89, the moon:** Night's moon lamp 5.0 → 0.7, a crescent's light (13 % lit). Midnight is untouched (M6).
- **R90, Night → Midnight goes up the falls** (design review §4 "V → VI"): the baked flight climbed straight through
  the south wall 4 m behind the camera. `FLIGHT_OVERRIDES['night>midnight']`: low across the lagoon, up beside the falls'
  left edge, over the lip, then the climb east. Lowest clearance over a wall: 6.8 m; Blue hour → Night: 28.8 m
  (`flight-clear.ts`). Over the lip the camera looks north to the coast, so that flight now draws the arch, the stacks
  and the cove pack (`ARCH_SEEN_ON`, `COVE_SEEN_ON`; their framing tests asked for it).
- Verdict: look 68 %, behaviour (static) 75 %. The bowl reads as an enclosure of columns; its wall is too even a
  texture of thin verticals.

### Task 2: the falls in two tiers (9557753)
- **R91:** the bowl's three front rows break off at 15.4 / 16.0 / 16.5 m in the alcove (±6.5°): a stepped ledge. The
  upper tier (two ribbons, 2.4 and 0.7 m wide at the foot) falls 21 m from the rim onto it; the lower tier (four
  ribbons, 0.8–3.9 m, wet rock between) falls 15 m into the lagoon. Total 36.9 m (Litlanesfoss: about 30 m).
- Each ribbon carries its own drop, so the streaks accelerate from each lip (√(2gh): 20 m/s at the upper tier's foot,
  17 m/s at the lower's). The lower tier arrives already white. Mist at both landings, sized to each; foam where the
  lower tier lands. `fallsLayout()` is pure (bowl.ts); the shader keeps M2's layers.
- Verdict (stills and a 150 ms frame strip): look 70 %, behaviour 78 %. The ledge reads as a hard dark block; ribbon
  edges too straight.

### Task 3: the corona (b81773d)
- **R92:** Night's curtains are made in `corona.ts` (Midnight keeps the baked ones). Every ray runs along a great circle
  toward one apex (azimuth −22°, elevation 64°: lower than the magnetic zenith on purpose, D2 styled, so the fan shows
  in a frame whose top is 29° up). Three curtains: the main fold (foot 21–27°), a higher one, a low band (9–12°) over
  the open west side. The lagoon mirrors them (owner aurora 3: the picture).
- **R93:** the shared rock material takes the aurora's green (`AURORA_GREEN` 0.003, 0.016, 0.010 linear, × the light
  uniform `aurora`), mostly on moss and on wet rock low down, in proportion to the sky the face sees; zero in hours
  without an aurora. The first value (twice this) painted the spray zone flat green.
- Verdict: aurora look 80 %, behaviour 78 % (it moves as before); walls taking green 70 %.

### Task 4: fireflies and reeds (1ba7555)
- **R94:** 72 fireflies, one sprite draw, eye only. Each on its own clock: a 0.75 s flash every 5.3–6.5 s (book §6:
  5.9 ± 0.6 s), a small rise while it flashes, 5 % glow between. 2.5–18 m out (first placed 3–30 m: too far, too
  small to read), 0.3–2 m over the water. Toward the pointer: where the pointer or a finger meets the water within
  40 m, they gather over about 4 s, each its own share of the way and place in a loose swarm, and drift off 3 s
  after it leaves. Reduced motion: no drift, the flashes stay.
- **R95:** 170 reeds, one instanced draw, eye only: clumps at the frame's sides and four short clumps at its foot (the
  first placement was a curtain across the view that hid the falls). Dark stems, the top 8 % a steady faint
  blue-green, a slow breath of sway (force 0).
- Verdict: fireflies 72 / 78, reeds 66 / 72 (look / behaviour).
